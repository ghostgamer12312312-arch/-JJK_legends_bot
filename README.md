🐉 Ultimate Dragon

**Bot Username:** @Dragon_Ball_legendsBot

Ultimate Dragon is a Dragon Ball themed RPG adventure bot for Telegram. Train your warriors, build your team, travel across different worlds, collect powerful characters, and become the strongest fighter in the universe.

---

## 🌟 About The Bot

Ultimate Dragon is an interactive Dragon Ball RPG bot designed for fans who want to experience the Dragon Ball universe directly inside Telegram.

Players can explore different locations, recruit powerful fighters, collect rare characters through the gacha system, complete adventures, earn rewards, and build the ultimate team capable of defeating any opponent.

Whether you dream of becoming a Saiyan warrior, collecting legendary fighters, or creating the strongest team in existence, Ultimate Dragon provides a complete Dragon Ball gaming experience.

---

## 🎮 Main Features

### 🔍 Explore
Travel through different areas and discover new adventures, enemies, rewards, and hidden opportunities.

### 👤 Profile
View your complete player profile including level, power, currency, progress, and achievements.

### 🌍 Travel
Journey between different worlds and unlock new locations inspired by the Dragon Ball universe.

### 👥 Team
Build and manage your team of powerful warriors. Create the perfect squad for battles and adventures.

### 🎲 Gacha
Summon and collect fighters ranging from common warriors to legendary and ultra-rare characters.

### 🛒 Shop
Purchase useful items, upgrades, boosts, and special resources using your in-game currency.

### ⚔️ Battle
Challenge enemies and prove your strength. Earn rewards, experience, and valuable resources through combat.

### 🏆 Quests
Complete missions and objectives to gain experience, currency, exclusive items, and special rewards.
